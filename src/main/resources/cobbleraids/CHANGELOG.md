# CobbleRaids changelog

## 0.8.209 (2026-11-27)

### New
- Daily Boss: every day at 7 pm Eastern a renowned World Boss appears for up to 25 players. Everyone who deals it damage earns the raid reward and a chance to catch it.
- Renowned bosses carry one of 30 renown affixes and have a dark bronze look. Catch one and your Pokémon keeps its affix.
- The Raidio: the Raid Radar is now a pocket radio you tune by ear. Each tier plays its own tune that speeds up as you get closer. Craft it from iron, redstone, a note block, amethyst and a Cobblemon upgrade.
- After a win you can keep the boss in your personal boss shop, up to 5 at a time for 7 days each.

### Changed
- Every raid is now a duel raid: up to 8 players each fight their own copy of the boss, all wearing down one shared health pool. A raid lasts 10 minutes and each duel up to 4.
- Joining a raid spends one raid charge, given back if the raid never starts.
- Raid Points have daily caps: 300 a day from raids and 150 from towers, resetting at 00:00 UTC.
- Raid screens now open as Snobblemon screens and fill your whole window. You no longer need the WebGUI mod; update to Snobblemon Client 2.25.0.
- Wild raids spawn on natural ground near your height, never inside land claims, and announcements name the biome instead of coordinates.

### Fixed
- Draining moves, Leech Seed, recoil, Counter, Mirror Coat and similar moves now work properly against raid bosses.
- Big raid bosses no longer spawn stuck inside trees or under low ceilings.
